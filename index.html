HOW TO PLAY

Move — WASD / left stick  ·  Look — mouse / drag right side
Jump — Space  ·  Sprint — Shift (burns hunger faster)
Attack / Shoot — Left click with a weapon selected
Break block — Left click with a block selected (hold to crack it)
Place block — Right click with a block selected
Hotbar — keys 1–9 or tap a slot
THE FORGE (F) — sketch any weapon on the 16×16 grid. The game reads your drawing: shape = weapon type (long & thin = staff/sword, fat head = hammer, arc = bow), colors = element. Red/orange → fire (burns trees, scorches & craters ground), cyan/white → frost (freezes mobs solid, ice spikes), yellow/gold → storm (lightning from the sky), green → venom (poison clouds), purple → void (implosion crater), brown/grey → earth (shockwave boulders, cracked ground). Even 4+ pixels of one bright color take over the whole weapon — a wooden staff with a gold tip is a STORM staff. Every element works with every weapon type, melee included: swing a venom sword and it leaves a poison cloud. And the name is law: name it with an element word (Fire, Frost, Storm, Venom, Void, Earth) or a type word (Staff, Sword, Axe, Hammer, Bow) and the weapon becomes exactly that — a Fire Staff always shoots fireballs, a Frost Sword always fires ice shards, no mismatches ever. Then it builds your sketch as a real 3D voxel weapon in your hand.
Survival — hunger always drains. Hunt animals for meat, shake berry bushes, eat or starve. Night brings husks, slimes and spiders. Kill things, score points, see how many days you last.
